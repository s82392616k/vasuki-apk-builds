Working

Paint over the thing you want gone, then tap Erase with AI. The model rebuilds what was behind it. Tap mode selects by color region.

Draw the rough shape, add what it is, then Make it real. The AI keeps your layout and turns it into a photograph. Use Trace photo to sketch over a reference.

AI keeps your subject sharp and rebuilds the background with matching light and shadows.

AI enhance fixes exposure, colour and softness in one pass. Auto tone and sharpen work offline.

AI connection

The generative tools (AI erase, sketch to photo, backdrop, enhance) need one image model key. Everything else works offline.

Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. It is saved only in this browser and sent straight to Google. Nothing else sees it.
